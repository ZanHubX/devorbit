ALote

03 OVERVIEW

Making opportunity easier to discover.

ALote is a Myanmar-focused job discovery platform built around a simpler way of finding and exploring relevant opportunities.

The experience focuses on reducing unnecessary friction between a user and the information they actually need — helping job seekers browse, understand and explore opportunities through a clear digital interface.

04 THE CHALLENGE

Turning scattered opportunities into a clearer experience.

01
Information clarity

Present job information in a way that is easier to scan and understand.

02
Discovery

Make it easier for users to explore opportunities without unnecessary complexity.

03
Practical usability

Keep the interface focused on useful actions rather than decorative complexity.

05 PROJECT OBJECTIVES
01

Simplify discovery

Create a straightforward path from opening the platform to discovering relevant opportunities.

02

Improve clarity

Establish stronger hierarchy so important job information can be understood quickly.

03

Build trust

Use a consistent interface and structured presentation to create a more professional experience.

04

Keep it practical

Prioritize useful functionality and usability over unnecessary visual complexity.

06 WORKFLOW

From product direction to working experience.

The project can be understood as a structured progression from defining the experience to implementing and refining the interface.

01
DISCOVER

Understand the product

Establish the purpose of the platform, its users and the information that needs to be surfaced.

↗
02
STRUCTURE

Organize the experience

Define the information hierarchy, navigation and relationships between the major product areas.

↗
03
DESIGN

Build the interface

Translate the product direction into a clean, consistent and practical UI / UX system.

↗
04
DEVELOP

Turn the design into a product

Implement the responsive web experience and connect the interface with the application architecture.

↗
05
REFINE

Polish the experience

Review interactions, spacing, hierarchy and responsiveness to create a more cohesive final experience.

↗

09 DESIGN APPROACH

Less decoration. More direction.

The interface is built around a restrained visual system where typography, spacing and hierarchy carry most of the experience.

Instead of overwhelming users with visual elements, the design gives important information room to breathe and keeps primary actions recognizable.

03 Consistency

04 Simplicity

ZaySai

03 OVERVIEW

Making seller operations easier to manage.

ZaySai is a SaaS platform for online sellers, built around a connected workflow for managing products, orders, store settings and customer-facing storefront experiences.

The experience focuses on reducing operational friction for sellers by bringing product management, order handling, store configuration and customer ordering into a clearer digital workflow.

04 THE CHALLENGE

Turning scattered seller tasks into a clearer connected workflow.

01
Operational clarity

Present seller information and actions in a way that is easier to scan, understand and manage.

02
Workflow

Make it easier for sellers to manage products, orders and store operations without unnecessary complexity.

03
Practical operations

Keep the platform focused on useful actions rather than decorative complexity.

05 PROJECT OBJECTIVES
01

Simplify seller operations

Create a straightforward path from opening the platform to managing core seller operations.

02

Improve workflow clarity

Establish stronger hierarchy so important product and order information can be understood quickly.

03

Connect the workflow

Use a consistent interface and structured workflow to create a more reliable seller experience.

04

Keep it scalable

Prioritize useful functionality and usability over unnecessary visual complexity.

06 WORKFLOW

From product direction to a connected seller experience.

The project can be understood as a structured progression from defining the seller workflow to implementing the platform and refining the product experience.

01
DISCOVER

Understand seller needs

↗

↗
03

↗
04

↗
